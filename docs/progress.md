## Explainable risk meter

- Added an authenticated deterministic risk-meter endpoint and report for Stock Analysis and Portfolio/Risk Analysis. It uses the configured historical provider, validated daily candles, existing indicator/performance calculations, and optional owner-scoped portfolio context. It does not create orders or trade automatically.
- Formula: start at 50; close vs SMA20 contributes +12/-12, close vs EMA20 +8/-8, MACD vs signal +12/-12, and period return contributes +10 at >=5%, +5 above 0% and below 5%, 0 at 0%, -5 below 0% and above -5%, or -10 at <=-5%. RSI14 contributes -8 at >=70, +6 at >=60, +3 at >=55, 0 above 45 and below 55, -3 above 40 through 45, -6 from 30 through 40, and -8 below 30. Instrument annualized volatility contributes -10 above 60%, -5 above 40% through 60%, 0 above 20% through 40%, or +2 at/below 20%. Optional portfolio risk adds -6/-3 for volatility above 60%/40%, -8/-4 for drawdown above 30%/15%, and -12/-8/-4 for holding concentration above 50%/35%/20%. Clamp to 0–100; BUY >=65, HOLD 36–64, SELL / REDUCE EXPOSURE <=35.
- Confidence is 70% core-signal completeness and 30% directional agreement (HIGH >=80%, MODERATE >=60%, otherwise LOW). Fewer than three of five valid core signals suppresses the score. Risk is HIGH if instrument/portfolio volatility >=40%, drawdown >=25%, or holding allocation >=40%; MODERATE at volatility >=20%, drawdown >=10%, or allocation >=20%; otherwise LOW when risk data exists, or UNKNOWN. Stale/unknown-freshness data never produces an actionable recommendation.
- The report exposes validated values, historical source, candle timestamp/freshness, top three factors, bullish/bearish evidence, limitations, and conditions that could change the assessment. Gemini is optional and can only explain the calculated fields; missing key, provider error, or malformed response does not alter the score.
- Hardened the optional Gemini risk explanation: the response must repeat the deterministic recommendation and any numeric value it states must match verified assessment fields (plus indicator-label numbers). Unverified numbers or a changed recommendation return an explanation error while preserving the deterministic result.
